# A fresh space for your ideas.

Welcome to **hosea**: an open-source desktop workspace for your models, tools, and agents.

- A quiet black-and-white design, original open-arch logo, and locally bundled Geist type.
- Light, dark, and system appearance with adjustable text size.
- A simpler connections hub for your own models and services.
- Local AI through Ollama, with no cloud API key required.

Your workspace is local. Bring your own model keys and choose the tools your agents can use.
